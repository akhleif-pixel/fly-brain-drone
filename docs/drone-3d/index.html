    Build guide

    Do the steps in this order; each one only needs what came before it. Tap a step to see it in 3D. Holes are named like [N8]: column letter A–P, row 1–14, read on the parts side with the front of the drone at the top.

    We only bought red wire. Mark each wire's ground end with a black marker or a strip of tape before soldering; the colors here are only to tell wires apart.

      How everything connects

      Each line is one wire. Pins on the ESP32 are named as printed on the board; J1 is the header with 3V3 at the top, J3 the header with GND at the top. Check them against your board's silkscreen before soldering.

      Driver board maps

      Two views of the same board. The solder side is a mirror image of the parts side, so check which one you are looking at before you solder. Dark rings are holes in use.

      Wire list

      Lengths are measured along each wire's path in the model, plus 10 mm to strip and solder. Cut them a little long and trim.

      #WireFromToLength

      Pin choices follow docs/wiring.md: motors on GPIO 21, 47, 14 and 38; MPU6050 on 41, 42 and 40. On some ESP32-S3 camera boards GPIO 38 and 40 also go to an SD card slot. Leave the SD slot empty, or move those two signals to GPIO 1 and 2.
