# Frame

3D-printable parts. Put STL/STEP files here.

- Frame for four 8.5 mm motors, X layout, thin walls (1.5–2 mm), PLA or PETG
- Prop guards for 55 mm props
- Camera mount, angled slightly forward, with foam against vibration
- Battery holder (battery about 40 × 20 × 7 mm) and landing feet
- Room for the 4 × 6 cm perfboard (cut smaller) and the ESP32-S3 CAM board (roughly 6 × 2.5 cm)

Target: whole drone under 40 g. Weigh each part.
